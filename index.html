About

I'm a security practitioner in the making — someone who learns by doing, not just studying. My approach is simple: build the lab, run the attack, read the logs, write it up.

My foundation is in blue team and cloud security — SOC workflows, CloudTrail forensics, SIEM deployment. But I'm deliberately expanding into offensive security: working through TryHackMe's Jr Pentester path, learning Burp Suite, web app vulnerabilities, and privilege escalation hands-on.

I'm also Author #2 on a cybersecurity research paper currently under review at Elsevier JNCA — serving as the sole cybersecurity domain lead on an IoT botnet detection study, handling MITRE ATT&CK mapping and adversarial robustness validation.

Most breaches aren't exotic. They're a misconfigured S3 bucket, a forgotten access key, one too many permissions. I practice finding those — and now I'm learning to think like the person who exploits them too.
